# Reports

`milestone-1-verification.md` is the tracked evidence index. Large run outputs,
models, predictions, and exports remain under ignored `runs/` or external
storage and are referenced only by content ID and SHA-256.

Static reviewed reports may be committed here. Per-run outputs live under the
ignored `runs/` or `reports/runs/` directories and are referenced by immutable
hash/ID rather than committed with model weights.
